We bridge the gap between the demand and supply of petroleum products

Logistics

Refuel Energy offers a framework for transporting oil from production sites to demand regions, To do this we leverage on a robust and comprehensive supply chain that includes maritime transport and storage sites.

Haulage of petroleum products is an absolute priority, Our service personnel and drivers are skilled and experienced professionals capable of identifying dangerous dispatch and delivery situations at every point in time.

At our core, we ensure that the quantity loaded is equal to the quantity delivered.

Retail

Refuel Energy offers a retail service that provides small volumes of Automotive Gas Oil (AGO) directly to end consumers in Homes, Companies, Manufacturing Industries and Hotels in Nigeria.

We supply in bulk, and you can buy any volume of diesel fuel across 36 states in Nigeria for their generators, engines and diesel-operated cars, as well as their tank farms and other designated storage facilities.

We can independently source refined products from direct importers through our local, global networks, and operate as a partner for delivery on an ex-depot basis.
We supply a quality product at competitive prices within the West African market 24/7/365 promptly.

Our product distribution mechanism comprises custom-made truck tanks which have an advantage over off-the-shelf branded vehicles in their adaptability and cost. For this reason, our clients enjoy a high level of satisfaction and our drivers supply on time to meet up with their needs.

Our priority is ensuring that our customers have their diesel needs met on request and on-demand, with timely delivery.

SHIPPING AND MARINE LOGISTICS

We are strategically positioned to offer our clients customised solutions, which includes unique integrated offshore marine logistics vessel services ranging from Vessel Chartering to Vessel Management. Refuel’s shipping team is able to ensure the 24-hour logistics support that is crucial to service shipping requirements in a professional and cost effective manner.

Since inception, Refuel has routinely chartered large tankers and have strategic technical partnerships with local and international companies of high reputation, we offer a large array of multipurpose vessels which include: Marine Tankers , Supply Vessels (PSV, FSIV, OSV), Fast Crew Boats (FCB), Barges, Anchor Handling Tug and Supply Vessel (AHTS), etc.

We are positioned to go the extra mile to meet and surpass clients and regulatory requirements. We are fully compliant with all regulatory requirements, do not compromise on Standards; thus, our shipping team ensures that all vessels in our employ comply completely with the appropriate shipping safety conventions worldwide and are fully approved to undertake the proposed delivery.
