About Refuel Energy

Refuel Energy is a leading oil and gas, energy service organisation established in 2012 with active operations in the downstream sector and a presence in diverse locations across Nigeria.
Our primary focus is the trading, marketing, and supply of oil and gas products for the facilitation of power generation and distribution across Nigeria.

We have developed innovative and efficient ways of enhancing our client’s businesses through our operations within an integrated energy group, with a clear vision for the future energy requirements in our customers’ ecosystem.

What refuel brings to the client is a well thought out process and pragmatic approach of bridging not just the demand and supply of energy resources required to power our clients business but a commitment to achieving this in the most efficient and timely manner while ensuring the integrity of the products, taking the burden away from our customers.

At Refuel we consolidate our relationships with our partners and staff and we rely on them to complete each delivery successfully. We have formed a proficient management team as well as competent staff that has the capacities of facing and resolving the diverse demands of the business and we believe in the importance of dedicated service, driven by impactful communication.

A major strength of our organization is that our logistics and engineering teams are constantly involved in imaginative and creative methods of innovation combining new technologies with skills and patience coupled with audacity and rigour as well as respect for our clients and each other.

Refuel has matched its growth with the continuous development of its 3 key assets; people, products, and processes to continue to provide impeccable services for its customers.

Our Services

  • Logistics

    Refuel Energy offers a framework for transporting oil from production sites to demand regions, To do this we leverage on a robust and comprehensive supply chain that includes maritime transport and storage sites.

  • Retail

    Refuel Energy offers a retail service that provides small volumes of Automotive Gas Oil (AGO) directly to end consumers in Homes, Companies, Manufacturing Industries and Hotels in Nigeria.

  • Marine

    We are strategically positioned to offer our clients customised solutions, which includes unique integrated offshore marine logistics vessel services ranging from Vessel Chartering to Vessel Management.
